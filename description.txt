[h1] Calamity Overhaul Mod [/h1]
[h2] [url=https://calamity-overhaul.cc]Mod Wiki[/url] [/h2]

This is a large content expansion. It adds more systems and story scenes, and also reworks a lot of what is already in the game.

[h2] Open Source & Community [/h2]
The mod is open source on [url=https://github.com/hocha113/CalamityOverhaul]Github[/url]. File issues, leave suggestions, or help with translations.

[h2] Play Tips [/h2]
-Make a new world after enabling the mod. It adds terrain that only appears in new maps.
-Grab [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2619954303&searchtext=] Recipe Browser Mod [/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2669644269&searchtext=] Boss Checklist Mod [/url] as well. They make the extra content easier to find.
-Try [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3316697363&searchtext=Calamity] Calamity Entropy Mod [/url]! We have crossover content coming.
-And try the FTB team's [url=https://www.mcmod.cn/modpack/526.html] Stone Block modpack series! [/url] I love you all. Without you I would never have gotten into modding.
